ROYAL PALACE WEDDING — CINEMATIC V4

1. Open index.html in Chrome or on your phone.
2. Add your couple photo as assets/images/couple.jpg
3. Add gallery photos: assets/images/gallery1.jpg, gallery2.jpg, gallery3.jpg
4. Add music as assets/music/wedding.mp3 (music starts after guest taps Open).
5. Edit names, date, ceremony schedule and venue in index.html.
6. In the script near the bottom, change WEDDING_DATE and WHATSAPP_NUMBER.
7. Change the Google Maps link in the element with id="maps".
8. Upload the entire folder to your hosting. The page works without a build step.

This is a sample demo. Sample names and venue are fictional placeholders. No copyrighted music or photos are bundled.
