ROYAL BLUE & GOLD BUDDHIST WEDDING
Open index.html in Chrome.
Images are separated in assets/images:
 - rotating-mandala.png (animated independently in CSS)
 - royal-blue-background.png
 - gold-divider.png
Edit the DATA object near the bottom of index.html to change names, date, venue, map and WhatsApp.
Optional music: add your own MP3 at assets/music/wedding.mp3.
The sample wedding names, venue and date are placeholders.
Mobile swipe, page-turn, autoplay, countdown, and RSVP are supported.
